Welcome

Follow the steps in order. Workit remembers where you are, so you can stop and come back any time.

    ES accounts

    Only you can see this page. Create an account for each ES, then send them the welcome message. They choose their own password and set up their own copy of Workit, so their students and work stay in their own Google Drive.

    Students

    Copy rows from Excel or Google Sheets in this order: First name, Last name, Grade, City, Sem 1 classes, Sem 2 classes. Click a cell and paste; the rows fill in below it. Separate classes with commas. Sem 1 is LP1 to LP4 and Sem 2 is LP5 to LP8. Type (Accel) after an accelerated class, for example Algebra 1A H (Accel): it needs 2 samples per LP. Leave Sem 2 blank to use Sem 1 with A changed to B (the grey text shows the result); accelerated and one-semester classes like Econ are left out, so type Sem 2 whenever it differs. To remove a student, click ✕ on their row. Nothing changes until you click Save students.

    Review

    Why does it need a redo? Pick all that apply, and type your own reason if you like. The student sees it the next time they sign in.

    LP dates

    Students can only enter a weekday inside the LP they upload to. Leave an LP blank for no date limit.

    These are the school’s LP dates. They’re the same for every ES and fill in by themselves, so there’s nothing to change.
