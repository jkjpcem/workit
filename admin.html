Students

Copy rows from Excel or Google Sheets in this order: First name, Last name, Grade, City, Sem 1 classes, Sem 2 classes. Click a cell and paste; the rows fill in below it. Separate classes with commas. Sem 1 is LP1 to LP4 and Sem 2 is LP5 to LP8. Type (Accel) after an accelerated class, for example Algebra 1A H (Accel): it needs 2 samples per LP. Leave Sem 2 blank to use Sem 1 with A changed to B (the grey text shows the result); accelerated and one-semester classes like Econ are left out, so type Sem 2 whenever it differs. To remove a student, click ✕ on their row. Nothing changes until you click Save students.
