Welcome

Follow the steps in order. WiSH remembers where you are, so you can stop and come back any time.

    ES accounts

    Only you can see this page. Create an account for each ES, and WiSH emails them a welcome message from your account with the link, their username and a temporary password. They choose their own password and set up their own copy of WiSH, so their students and work stay in their own Google Drive.

    Students

    Copy rows from Excel or Google Sheets in this order: First name, Last name, Grade, City, Sem 1 classes, Sem 2 classes, Student email, Parent emails. Names stay put while you scroll right. Put several parent emails in one box, separated by commas. Click a cell and paste; the rows fill in below it. Separate classes with commas. Sem 1 is LP1 to LP4 and Sem 2 is LP5 to LP8. Type (Accel) after an accelerated class, for example Algebra 1A H (Accel): it needs 2 samples per LP. Leave Sem 2 blank to use Sem 1 with A changed to B (the grey text shows the result); accelerated and one-semester classes like Econ are left out, so type Sem 2 whenever it differs. Set Status to Inactive for a student who left: they can still sign in and see their work, but can’t turn in new work and get no reminders. To remove a row completely, clear its cells. Nothing changes until you click Save students.

    Student Classes

    Type a class and press Enter. Add (Accel) after an accelerated class, or click a class to switch Accel on or off: it needs 2 samples per LP. Leave Sem 2 empty to use Sem 1 with A changed to B (shown in grey). Accelerated and one-semester classes like Econ are left out of that, so add Sem 2 classes whenever they differ.

    Trash

    LP meetings

    Booking pages

    In Google Calendar, make a booking page (Create → Appointment schedule) and paste its link here. Families get a “Book your LP meeting” button in WiSH, and your reminder emails can use {booking link}.

    One page works all year: WiSH puts each booking in an LP by its date. At the start of each LP, change the page’s available dates in Google.

    A different page for one LP (optional)

    WiSH always counts bookings made on a booking page. Add words here to also count events you put on your calendar yourself whose title has one of these words (separate with commas). Put “LP3” in a title to count it for LP3.
